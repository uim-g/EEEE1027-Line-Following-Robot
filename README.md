# EEEE1027 Line-Following Robot

University of Nottingham Malaysia

EEEE1027 — Applied Electrical and Electronic Engineering Construction Project

## Project aim

I am building and testing a line-following robot for my EEEE1027 construction project. I use an Arduino UNO, a motor driver and infrared sensors. I keep my design decisions, wiring and trial results here.

## My progress

In Week 1 I helped assemble the chassis, worked on the countdown and adjusted the left and right PWM. I observed the car stop after 10 seconds with a slight left drift. My teammate recorded a straight run at a different PWM setting.

In Week 2 we built an infrared sensor on a breadboard and transferred it to Veroboard. We stopped pursuing the single-sensor setup after it repeatedly overshot sharp corners. We mounted three sensors and logged full laps of 32 s, 27 s and 19.1 s. I still need to confirm the settings used for the shortest lap and check the distance estimate against a measured track length.
