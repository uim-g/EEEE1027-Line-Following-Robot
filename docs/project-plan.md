## How I track progress

I use To Do for tasks I have yet to start, Doing for work I am carrying out or verifying, and Done when I have the evidence I need. I record blockers on the relevant issue.

I use Project Week to group coursework and Roadmap to track start dates, finish dates and durations. I record confirmed dates first and check outstanding dates against the lab records and Moodle.
